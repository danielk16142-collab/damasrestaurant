# Home services: landscaping, maintenance, cleaning, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, pools

The customer is often on a phone, often in a hurry, and deciding between 3 local companies. The site has to earn trust in seconds and make calling or requesting a quote effortless. Premium here means **clean, confident and fast**, not editorial drama. A well-made site is itself the differentiator in this market.

## Pages
- **Home:** a clear promise plus the service area in the hero ("Landscape design & maintenance in Austin and Round Rock"), a call button and a quote button, services, recent work, reviews, the process, service areas, final call to action.
- **Services:** an overview plus one page per core service (what's included, typical timeline, a "starting at" price if the client is comfortable, FAQs, related work).
- **Our work / Projects:** a gallery filterable by service. Before/after sliders only with the homeowner's permission.
- **Service areas:** one page per town or city they really serve (unique text about local conditions, not copy-paste). This is the biggest local SEO lever.
- **About:** owner story, team photos, licences, insurance, years in business, guarantees.
- **Reviews:** real reviews linked to Google or Yelp. Never invented ones.
- **Contact / Get a quote.**

## Mapping from the template
| Template | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Properties + filters | Projects gallery, filtered by service/area |
| Property detail | Service page or project case study (facts bar: duration, area size, season) |
| Neighbourhoods | Service areas |
| Buyers / Sellers | Residential / Commercial (or Maintenance plans / One-off projects) |
| Mortgage estimator | Simple cost estimator ("lawn size × frequency"), clearly labelled as an estimate, or remove |
| Enquiry form intents | quote, maintenance plan, emergency repair, commercial, general question |

## Conversion
- A **sticky click-to-call button on mobile** plus "Get a free quote" in the nav. Show the phone number in the header on desktop.
- **Quote form, multi-step:** service → property type (home/commercial) → size or scope → zip/address → preferred timing → contact details. An optional photo upload is a strong feature, but it needs a real backend (Wix, Formspree with files, etc.), so keep it a placeholder until then.
- Emergency services (plumbing, HVAC): show "24/7" and the phone number above everything else.
- Show seasonal offers and maintenance plans if the business sells them (recurring revenue for them, a strong call to action).

## Design and motion
- Motion: **calm to signature**. Keep reveals short so content never waits on animation. There's no intro/preloader in this industry.
- Photography does the selling: real crews, real finished work, golden-hour exteriors. Avoid stock photos of smiling handymen.
- Typography: a confident sans or slab/grotesk pairing usually fits better than high-fashion serifs, though a design-led landscaping firm can go editorial.
- Trust strip near the top: licensed & insured, years in business, star rating plus review count, guarantee.

## Content rules
- No invented reviews, badges, awards or certifications. Use "sample" placeholders only in demos, clearly marked.
- Prices: "starting at" or ranges only when the client approves; otherwise "free quote".
- Licence numbers where the trade requires them.

## Integrations
- Quote requests into their CRM or job software. Common tools: Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Yardbook. Ask which they use; many have embeddable request forms and online booking.
- Google Business Profile link, reviews widget (or static curated reviews with links).
- Call tracking is optional.

## SEO
- LocalBusiness (or HomeAndConstructionBusiness / specific types like Plumber, RoofingContractor) structured data on every page, with `areaServed`.
- Service × city pages with unique content, and consistent name/address/phone everywhere.
- Fast mobile performance: keep hero images tight and lazy-load galleries.

## Hosting
Usually **Hostinger** (domain + business email + site in one bill) or Cloudflare Pages. Use Wix Headless only if they want to self-edit projects or run bookings in Wix.
