# Hospitality: hotels, boutique inns, villas, vacation rentals, retreats

The goal is **direct bookings**, which saves the property the 15–25% commission from booking platforms. The site has to feel like the stay itself and make "Check availability" the easiest thing to do.

## Pages
- **Home:** an immersive hero (photo or video), an availability bar (dates + guests) in or just below it, the concept, rooms highlights, experiences, location, reviews, offers.
- **Rooms & suites:** a filterable list (guests, bed type, view), then a room detail page.
- **Experiences / Amenities:** spa, dining, activities, local guides.
- **Location:** map, getting here, nearby attractions (neighbourhood-page pattern).
- **Offers / Packages**, **Weddings & events** (if relevant), **About / Story**, **Contact**.

## Mapping from the template
| Template | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Properties + filters | Rooms & suites (filters: guests, bed, view, rate from) |
| Property detail | Room page: facts bar (size, guests, beds, view), gallery + lightbox, amenities, sticky "Book this room" |
| Neighbourhoods | Location & experiences |
| Buyers / Sellers | Stay / Events & weddings |
| Mortgage estimator | "Rates from", or a simple stay-cost preview (clearly an estimate), or remove |
| Hero search | Dates + guests availability bar that hands off to the booking engine |
| Form intents | stay enquiry, wedding, corporate/group, press, general question |

## Conversion
- **The booking engine is the call to action.** Pass the dates and guests from the availability bar through to the engine's URL. Until connected, the booking demo is a clearly labelled placeholder.
- "Book direct" benefits strip: best rate, free breakfast, flexible cancellation (only what the property really offers).
- Sticky "Book" on mobile.

## Design and motion
- Motion: **signature to expressive** for boutique and luxury (slow image reveals, horizontal room galleries), **calm** for family or budget properties.
- Imagery is everything: light, texture and the view. Use large galleries, and let each room show its own character.
- Consider an **intro/signature moment** tied to the place (a horizon line for a coastal property, doors opening for a heritage building).

## Content rules
- Rates, availability, amenities and policies come from the property. Don't invent them, even "from" prices.
- Real reviews only, with the source (Google, Tripadvisor).
- Accessibility information for rooms and public spaces.

## Integrations
- Booking engine / channel manager: Cloudbeds, Mews, SiteMinder, Little Hotelier, Guesty/Hostaway (vacation rentals), or Wix Hotels. Ask which one they use; most support a deep link with the dates.
- Restaurant or spa booking may be separate tools; link to them.

## SEO
- Hotel / LodgingBusiness / VacationRental structured data, with amenities, check-in/out times and address.
- Room pages as real pages (not only a slider) so each can rank and be shared.

## Hosting
Cloudflare Pages or Vercel Pro for speed with international guests; Wix Headless if they want Wix Hotels/Bookings and self-editing.
