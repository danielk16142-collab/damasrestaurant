# Healthcare: doctors, dentists, clinics, aesthetics

Covers independent doctors and dentists as well as multi-doctor clinics. For a solo practitioner, merge "Doctors" into About (one strong bio page) and keep the site short: Home, Treatments, About the doctor, New patients, Contact/Book.

Trust beats drama here, so default to the **calm** motion level, high legibility (a larger body size, strong contrast) and warm, human photography of the real team and rooms.

- **Pages:** Home, Treatments/Services + a treatment detail page (what it is, who it's for, what to expect, recovery, FAQs), Doctors (bio, qualifications, board certifications, memberships), New patients (first visit, insurance, fees, forms), Locations & hours, Contact/Book.
- **The property detail page maps to a treatment or doctor page:** hero, a facts bar (duration, recovery, sessions), sticky "Book a consultation" panel, gallery, FAQ.
- **Booking is the main call to action.** Keep the demo as a placeholder until the practice's real scheduling system is connected (they often already have one; ask which).
- **Privacy:** the enquiry form must **not** ask for symptoms, conditions or other health details. Collect only name, contact details, preferred time and the type of appointment, and add a note like "Please don't include medical information". Health data needs a compliant backend (e.g. HIPAA in the US, GDPR special-category data in the EU), which a marketing form and generic CRM are not. Raise this in the plan and let the practice decide.
- **Claims:** no guaranteed outcomes, and no before/after images without consent and a disclaimer. Invented stats or testimonials are not acceptable even as placeholders on a medical site; use clearly marked "sample" text or leave them out.
- **Trust signals:** credentials, affiliations, years in practice, real reviews (linked to the source), accessibility info (parking, step-free access), emergency disclaimer ("For emergencies call 911").
- **Local SEO matters most:** `seo-audit`, plus MedicalClinic/Physician structured data and consistent name/address/phone across the site.
- **Accessibility is a must:** run `web-design-guidelines` before handover.
